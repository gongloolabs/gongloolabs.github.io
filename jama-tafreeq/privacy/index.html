Privacy Policy for Jama Tafreeq

Jama Tafreeq: Expense Tracker · Effective 4 October 2026

Jama Tafreeq is a personal expense and budget tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your financial records stay on your phone. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no permission to use the internet.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Expenses and income: amount, category, date and the note you type or say.
  • Your categories, their icons and voice keywords.
  • Budgets and settings, such as your currency, decimal places, voice language and the day your month starts.

The developer never receives this information. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Voice input

When you use voice Quick add, the app asks your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's) to turn what you say into text. The app does not record or store audio; it only receives the resulting text, which you review before saving. Speech recognition is provided by that service under its own privacy policy, for example Google's Privacy Policy.

Assistants such as Gemini

On Android 16 and later, the app can let an assistant on your phone, such as Gemini, add an expense for you or list your expense categories, but only when you ask the assistant to. The assistant then sees the details needed for that request (for example the amount and note you gave it, and your category names). This is handled by the assistant under its own privacy policy. The app does not share anything with an assistant on its own.

Backups

  • Export and Import. You can export all your data to a file and import it later. The file is saved wherever you choose, and only you decide where it goes and who it is shared with.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings and handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Exported backup files are separate files you control; delete them like any other file. Device backups can be managed in your Google account settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
